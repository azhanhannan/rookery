## (a) Where I agree

- **Rahim's mistake-to-puzzle loop with spaced intervals.** It's the same idea I pushed in Round 1, and his intervals (1/3/7/21 days) are better than my vague "spaced repetition". Two of us got there on our own, so it should go near the top.
- **Farid's variation tree and "Analyse deeper here".** My "Continue from here" only works if moves branch instead of overwriting the game. I'd also use multi-PV: when the top 3 lines are within 0.2 of each other, I'll stop panicking about an "Inaccuracy" that's really a matter of taste.
- **Aina's plain-words coach line.** I'm not a beginner, but "+1.4" with no reason doesn't teach me either. One sentence helps every level below Farid.
- **Rahim's themed puzzles, the threat arrow, and practice/rated split.** The red "what he threatened" arrow is exactly what I miss in blitz.

## (b) Where I disagree

- **Farid**, quick correction: I never asked for achievements. I asked for opening data from my own games and bulk import, which is close to your "explorer from my own games".
- **Rahim, engine hidden by default.** You're right about me. I do glance at the accuracy number and move on. But if I'm forced to self-review after *every* blitz game, I'll do it for three days and then go back to reviewing on Lichess. I play 10+ games a day, and I'd only properly review the rapid ones.
- **Aina, takebacks.** They're fine in casual games and harmful in rated ones. If takebacks were allowed in rated games, my bot rating would mean nothing, and "Maia one above my rating" sparring depends on that rating being honest.
- **Aina, hanging-piece glow.** I'd keep it Practice-only. In any game I'm trying to improve in, it does my blunder-check for me.
- **Farid, labels.** I don't want Brilliant/Great removed. I just want them rare and with the delta number next to them, which is your "Both" setting.

## (c) Compromises I'd accept

1. **New game dialog: Practice vs Rated.** That's Rahim's toggle. Takebacks, hints, the threat-check button and Aina's hanging-piece glow are allowed in Practice only. Rated stays clean.
2. **Self-review gate as a setting** ("Think first: off / rapid and slower only / always"), defaulting to *rapid and slower only*. My blitz stays fast, and my rapid losses get the slow treatment Rahim wants.
3. **Review display setting: Words / Numbers / Both.** Aina gets Words, Farid gets Numbers, and I'd use Both.

## (d) Final ranking

**Top 5 features**
1. **Mistake-to-puzzle loop** with spaced repetition (Puzzles → "My mistakes").
2. **Free analysis board** with a variation tree, position setup, multi-PV and "Continue from here" from Review.
3. **Bulk import** by Lichess username and from multi-game PGN files. Without it, items 1 and 4 have nothing to work on.
4. **Repertoire report on My games:** results per opening and the move where I usually go wrong.
5. **Practice/Rated split** in the New game dialog, covering takebacks, threat check and premoves (in Rated too).

**Top 3 cosmetics**
1. **Label badge on the board square** in Review, with a one-line caption and the eval delta under it.
2. **Two-colour arrows:** red for the opponent's threat and green for the better move, plus hand-drawn arrows with modifier colours on the Play board.
3. **Clock and sound polish:** clock turns red under 20s, tenths under 10s, a low-time tick, and a pack picker (wood / soft / silent).
