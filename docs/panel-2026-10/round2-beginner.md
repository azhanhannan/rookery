# Round 2: Aina (beginner, rated 500–700)

## (a) What I agree with

- **Coach Rahim's Practice vs Rated toggle.** This is exactly my takeback wish, but better organised. The **threat check** button is even better than my "hanging piece" glow: it shows me what *they* want to do, and that's the thing I never look at.
- **Rahim's plain-word captions and red threat arrow.** "This left your knight undefended" is the sentence I asked for. If the arrow is red for "their threat", I'd get it straight away.
- **Daniel and Rahim's mistake-to-puzzle loop.** Puzzles made from *my own* blunders sound way more motivating than random ones. I'd want them to feel short and friendly, though: "Remember this one? Find the move you missed."
- **Rahim's "hide rating" option and habit rings.** A losing streak hurts less if the screen shows "trained 4 days" instead of "−47".
- **Rahim's calmer label colours.** Ten labels was too many for me. Three is fine.

## (b) Where I disagree

- **Farid**, "eval in numbers, always" would scare me off. `+1.34 d24` looks like a spreadsheet. Please don't make that the default for everyone.
- **Farid**, the variation tree, multi-PV and NAG glyphs (`?!`, `$14`) are fine for you, but if I land on a review screen with brackets inside brackets, I close it. I know you said beginner features "sit on top of" your board, and I accept that for the *code*. I don't accept that the default *screen* has to look like that.
- **Coach Rahim**, I like self-review, but making it the default before I can see anything feels like homework after every lost game. When I've just lost I'm upset, and writing notes on moves I don't understand will make me skip Review altogether. I'd rather do *one* question: "Where do you think it went wrong?" Tap a move, then see the answer.
- **Daniel**, premoves and the opening repertoire report mean nothing to me yet. I don't know which opening I play. Please keep them out of my way.

## (c) Compromises I'd accept

- **A "Review style" setting chosen at sign-up: Simple / Standard / Full.** Simple gives captions, threat and better-move arrows, and key moments only. Full gives Farid's numbers, depth and tree. Everyone gets the same engine underneath.
- **Rahim's self-review, made light:** a single "guess the turning point" tap, skippable without guilt. A full self-review is opt-in, or switched on by a coach.
- **Analysis board**: fine as long as it's its own screen, reachable from a small "Explore this position" button, not dumped on me by default.

## (d) Final rankings

**Top 5 features**
1. Practice mode vs bots: takebacks, threat check, unrated (Rahim's version of my wish)
2. Plain-word coach caption on each mistake, plus "Try again" from that position
3. My-mistakes puzzles with gentle spaced repetition (Daniel/Rahim)
4. Daily puzzle and a days-trained streak on the home screen
5. Simple Review mode: key moments only, numbers hidden behind "Show details"

**Top 3 cosmetics**
1. A rook mascot that speaks the captions, so feedback feels kind, not judging
2. Red "their threat" and green "better move" arrows (Rahim)
3. Soft wood sound pack, a short skippable win celebration, and a loss screen that shows one good move first
